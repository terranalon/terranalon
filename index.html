Hermes

Hermes is a private, personal assistant used only by its owner, Alon Samocha. It connects to the owner's own Google account (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) to manage his calendar, read and send his email, and work with his files on his behalf.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
